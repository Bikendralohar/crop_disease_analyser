Our Mission

To democratize agricultural expertise by putting a high-accuracy laboratory in every farmer's pocket.

The Dataset

Trained on the Plant Disease Classification Merged Dataset dataset, containing over 76,000+ curated images across 88 health categories.

Project Methodology & Evolution
Data Preprocessing

Every image undergoes automated resizing to 224x224 pixels and pixel normalization. We employed advanced data augmentation (flips, rotations) to ensure accuracy across all lighting conditions.

Future Scope

Our next steps involve integrating real-time field sensing, IoT irrigation control, and expanding the model to recognize nutrient deficiencies in addition to diseases.

Technical Specifications
Neural Architecture

EfficientNetB3 utilizing "Compound Scaling" to balance depth, width, and resolution. Leverages ImageNet-1K pre-trained weights for advanced feature extraction in botanical pathology.

Analysis Pipeline

Integrated Flask-PyTorch backend with 224x224 RGB tensor normalization and Softmax-based multi-class probability distribution across 88 categories.
